Support Center

How can we help you?

Everything you need to know about booking, cancellations, and travel requirements.

How do I book a flight with SkyFlow?

Simply use the search widget in the hero section. Enter your departure, destination, and dates. Once you select a flight, proceed to the checkout to confirm your booking.

What is the baggage allowance for domestic flights?

For most domestic routes, we allow 15kg of check-in baggage and 7kg of cabin baggage. Premium members may be eligible for additional weight.

Can I cancel or reschedule my flight online?

Yes! Go to the "Manage Booking" section after logging in. You can modify your itinerary up to 24 hours before departure, subject to fare rules.

What travel documents do I need for international travel?

You will need a valid passport (minimum 6 months validity) and the necessary visas for your destination. Please check the local embassy requirements before booking.
